Buying and downloading

Products · Buying and downloading

All three versions at once: the Unreal Engine 5 pack, the Unity package and the raw files (heightmaps, textures, meshes). Frosthölm packs ship in their Unreal version for now.

Products · Buying and downloading

On the site you get the three versions and deal with me directly. On the marketplaces, their own terms and licences apply.

Products · Buying and downloading

Check your spam folder first. If nothing shows up within 10 minutes of your purchase, write to contact@kalysteon.com with your order email.

Products · Buying and downloading

Digital products aren't taken back once delivered. If a file is faulty or doesn't match its description, tell me: I fix or replace it, and if I can't, you get a refund.

Getting started

Stratum · Getting started

Stratum drives Gaea, Houdini, Unreal Engine and Unity; it does not replace them. You need your own licences for the ones in your pipeline.

Stratum · Getting started

Licences and updates

Stratum · Licences and updates

Free is for personal, non-commercial use. Indie and Studio include commercial use, and Studio covers a team within one studio. Details are on the Stratum page.

Stratum · Licences and updates

Stratum tells you in the app when a new version is out; download it from the Stratum page. A paid licence includes one year of updates, and the software keeps working after that.

Before starting

Commissions · Before starting

Three kinds of worlds: a custom terrain, a complete world, or a hero asset, delivered playable in Unreal Engine 5 or Unity.

Commissions · Before starting

Just tell me about your project: the game, the mood, what the player does there. References help, but the brief is five questions, no jargon.

Commissions · Before starting

Yes, mostly indie developers, from solo creators to studios with a budget.

Price and payment

Commissions · Price and payment

Every project gets its own quote, set together after the brief. It states the content, the price, the included revisions, the timeline and the rights.

Commissions · Price and payment

A 50% deposit to start, the remaining 50% at final approval, before the final files are delivered.

Commissions · Price and payment

Once work has started, the deposit covers the work done, and the previews already sent cannot be used.

During the project

Commissions · During the project

Usually 1 to 4 weeks depending on the scope. The exact timeline is confirmed in the quote, before any work starts.

Commissions · During the project

Set in the quote, usually 2 rounds on the shape and 2 on the details. Extra rounds are quoted separately.

Commissions · During the project

Everything set up in your engine, plus terrain files, FBX meshes and textures. Raw software project files are only included if agreed.

Commissions · During the project

Once fully paid, you get a commercial licence, and exclusivity if the quote includes it. I may show the work in my portfolio unless agreed otherwise.
