ANDREI BOBE

My story ↓ Get in touch
Age 13

I've dreamed of making games since I was a kid. At 13 I finally dove in for real and installed every engine I could get my hands on. The first one was Unity 3. I didn't understand much of it, so I moved on to CryEngine 3, which I pushed in every direction, starting project after project. Then came the old Unreal 3, the last one on the list.

The maps

From that point on I simply never stopped iterating. Whether the project was shared with friends or built entirely on my own, I always started it the same way: I began with the map. Back then it felt like the logical place to start. The result: I spent all my time on it and the project stopped before it was finished.

So I iterated on maps over and over again. I built them, I tore them down, I rebuilt them endlessly. The irony of the story is that all that fumbling turned into real skill. I learned level building, world layout and above all the craft of atmosphere, because that unique touch is what I love bringing. Speaking of which, here are my oldest recorded projects:

2020

Around 2020 I poured all of that obsession into Kalysteon. It became a catalogue of production-ready environment packs, made for developers who want quality at a fair price. I have now spent six years selling on Fab and the Unity Asset Store. Every pack is the result of accumulated time and of workflows refined by my damn perfectionism (not a quality, just so you know).

Today

Today my craft is environment art. I want a space to feel real and inhabited, and genuinely worth exploring. I'm not only after pretty renders: I want those renders to tell something. Worlds that hold up and make sense, for me, for your projects and for the people who walk through them.

Above all, I want to build unique moods that you are free to shape into your own vision. For that I wanted to vary my styles and learn other art directions. And it was through all that exploring that my tagline came to me:

Choose the sight of your world.Andrei

Where the name comes from
KALOS+EON=KALYSTEON

Kalos (καλός): Greek for beautiful. Also the root of calisthenics, kalos and sthenos, beauty and strength, the art of mastering form through the body alone.

Eon: an era, a geological age. The span of time that actually shapes a place.

That's how Kalysteon came to me. The T was already there in calisthenics, sitting between kalos and sthenos. I kept it as a natural bridge into eon. Nothing forced, it was in the root all along.
